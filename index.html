Our story

Our roots run deep.
Our perspective is new.

Wafadar Tobacco takes its name from the mountains — a quiet tribute to the landscapes, people and traditions that shape our identity.

Our vision brings that heritage into a contemporary pouch format. Familiar in spirit. Considered in design. A brand that looks forward without forgetting where it began.

What is Wafadar Tobacco?

Wafadar Tobacco is a naswar pouch brand rooted in Pakistani mountain culture. The visual identity and collection shown here represent our brand vision.

Who are these products for?

Naswar is a tobacco product intended only for adults of legal tobacco-use age. Tobacco and nicotine are addictive and carry serious health risks. This website does not sell tobacco.

Where can I find product information?

Use the arrow beside each product to explore it. For confirmed ingredients, nicotine content, pack sizes and availability, contact us directly.

Can I order from this website?

No. This is a brand presentation, not an online shop. No orders or payments are accepted here.
